## Work done
#### Spot welding at Sodion
- Continuation of spot welding of battery packs at Sodion.
- Completed sides were fitted with insulating sheets and ends taped with electrical tape for safety.
- Finished spot welding both battery packs. Left assembly and spot welding of battery packs onto the BMS.
- Utilised the handheld spot welder for the job while the spot welder machine was undergoing repair.
- Took spot welder machine apart to diagnose fault and identified it to be a fault in one of the capacitor modules. burn marks could be seen on the PCB near a solder joint whilst the capacitor looked fine, so we think likely the solder was the culprit. Will require replacement module for full repair and testing.
