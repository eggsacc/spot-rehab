## TL;DR

Continuation of spot welding of battery pack at Sodion with the help of gooner Wonje. Spot Welder Machine had a transistor fault and halted our progress.

## Work done
#### Spot welding at Sodion
- Continuation of spot welding of battery packs at Sodion.
- Completed sides were fitted with insulating sheets and ends taped with electrical tape for safety.
- Was close to 3/4 done with the spot welding when it suddenly broke. Error code 22 - Transistor fault
- about 30 mins of use, welding the nickel strips to negative terminals with no issues, all of the sudden a pop sound and smoky smell coming from the welding power supply. :(
- Tried to fix but to no avail, requesting help from the chinese helpline and they will help us with remote repair on tuesday afternoon.
- welding was halted for the day.
  
## Findings & data
- Diagnosis of the fault concluded in Electrode Fouling and Mushrooming, Thermal Saturation of the IGBTs, and Contact Pressure Drift resutlting in the transistor fault.
