## TL;DR
Started replicating the original cell-pack layout in CAD to make a 3D-printable spacer. The original spacing is surprisingly irregular — cells aren't evenly spaced, and even within a row they don't line up (the center cells sit slightly higher than the rest).

## Work done
#### Dimensioning the battery spacing
- Began measuring the original pack to reproduce the cell positions in CAD.

## Findings & data
- The original BD cell spacing is highly irregular — cells are unevenly spaced with awkward dimensions throughout. (Whoever dimensioned this pack must have been having a laugh.)
- Cells in the same row aren't collinear: the center cells sit slightly higher than the others in the row.

## Decisions
- Print the spacers in PC-CF. **Why:** PC-CF can withstand high temperatures and have much better inter-layer bond strength, maximizing rigidity and operational temperature limits.
